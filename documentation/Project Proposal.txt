Project Proposal

The IT Inventory Management System will provide a centralized way for a university to track, 
assign, and maintain electronic devices across departments and campus locations. 
The system will manage equipment such as laptops, desktop computers, smart boards, laptop carts, 
printers, routers, and switches. Through a simple graphical interface, authorized users will be 
able to securely sign in, identify their department, assign available devices, update device conditions, 
and return equipment. By supporting secure user access, due-date validation, device-status tracking, 
and complete CRUD inventory management, the system can improve accountability, reduce equipment loss, 
and help IT staff make informed decisions about repairs, replacements, and future technology needs.

This project is especially valuable to me because I work in IT for a local middle school in my area, 
where managing technology and equipment is an important part of supporting students and staff. My current 
experience has given me an understanding of the importance of accurate inventory records, reliable equipment 
assignments, and quick access to information when devices need maintenance or replacement. 
Developing this application will allow me to apply that experience while challenging myself to design a 
high-level management system capable of serving an entire university system. It will help me grow from 
managing technology in a school environment to planning a secure, organized, and scalable solution for a 
much larger and more complex organization.
